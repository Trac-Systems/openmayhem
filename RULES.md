# Mayhem Router Rules

Status: stub for Phase 0.

By joining Mayhem, participants consent to the router rules version recorded in the Mayhem contract. The full v1 rules text will be completed in task P1.8 and pinned by hash before gated contract operations are enabled.

Minimum draft commitments:

- Providers serve only registered enclaves and contract-listed model artifacts.
- Users and providers accept contract pricing, metering, receipts, and epoch settlement.
- Off-contract price negotiation is not authoritative.
- Abuse, false provenance, forged receipts, and non-service of required evidence are slashable or reputation-affecting events once those mechanisms are implemented.
- Local test resources and credentials are never committed.
